# Feature guides

Short help articles for LuLinDingo, one per file. They're written for parents and teachers, in plain language, and they describe what each feature does and how to use it. No setup or account is needed for any of them, and everything stays on the device.

## Getting started

- [Getting started](getting-started.md) — the first launch: name, choosing a level, and landing on the path.
- [Placement test](placement-test.md) — the optional quiz that finds a good starting level.

## How the app works

- [The learning path](learning-path.md) — units, lessons, and how things unlock.
- [Question types](question-types.md) — the core ways questions are asked (type, pick, follow the pattern).
- [Offline use and privacy](offline-and-privacy.md) — no account, works offline, data stays on the device.

## Rewards and motivation

- [Hearts](hearts.md) — lives that you lose on a wrong answer and that refill over time.
- [XP and points](xp-and-levels.md) — points earned for answers and finishing lessons.
- [Stars](stars.md) — one to three stars per lesson, based on how many you got right.
- [Streaks](streaks.md) — days in a row of practice.
- [Practice mode](practice-mode.md) — replay a finished lesson with no hearts at risk, and earn one back.
- [Daily quest board](daily-quest-board.md) — three goals a day that fill up as your child plays.
- [Dingo's Den](dingos-den.md) — spend earned points to decorate Dingo's world and dress Dingo up.

## Inside a lesson

- [Read aloud](read-aloud.md) — the app reads each question out loud, for early readers or tired eyes.
- [Show me how](show-me-how.md) — after a wrong answer, a visual walks through a way to work it out.
- [Story problems](story-problems.md) — math wrapped in a short story, plus division with remainders.
- [Equation puzzles](equation-puzzles.md) — fill in a missing number, or build a true equation from tiles.
- [Closest Wins](closest-wins.md) — an estimation challenge that rewards a good ballpark guess.

## Practice that adapts

- [Fact Vault](fact-vault.md) — quietly tracks which facts are shaky and brings them back to review.

## For grown-ups

- [Grown-Up Corner](grown-up-corner.md) — a parent dashboard behind a quick multiplication check.
- [Family profiles](family-profiles.md) — several children on one device, each with separate progress.
